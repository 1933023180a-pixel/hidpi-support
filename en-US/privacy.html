HiDPI · Privacy Policy

HiDPI processes connected-display names, display modes and screen arrangement locally to provide its display controls. It stores language and appearance preferences in the app’s local settings. The app does not transmit this information to the developer.

HiDPI contains no ads, analytics, account system, tracking SDK or cloud sync. It does not collect personal data or capture the contents of your screen. All display controls work without an internet connection.

Opening email support launches your chosen email app. If you send a message, the developer receives the email address and information you include and uses them to answer your request. Do not include passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Uninstalling HiDPI removes the application; you can also delete its local app settings using macOS. The developer does not sell your data. This policy applies to the Mac App Store edition and will be updated if its data practices change.

Opening Online Help requests a document in the selected language over HTTPS from hidpi.sylll.com, hosted on an Alibaba Cloud server in mainland China. No display information, preferences, account, device identifier or screen content is included. The network provider and hosting infrastructure necessarily process your IP address and connection metadata to deliver the document. HiDPI does not use cookies or retain help access logs; infrastructure providers may keep operational security records under their own policies. You can use bundled Offline Help instead, and display controls remain available offline.
