HiDPI · Help

Choose a display in the sidebar. Under Resolution, select a mode and click Apply Mode. Confirm within 15 seconds to keep it; otherwise HiDPI restores the previous mode. Available modes depend on macOS, your display and its connection. This edition does not create virtual displays or add unsupported resolutions.

Under Arrangement, select a secondary, non-mirrored display and place it to the left, right, above or below the main screen. Changes last for the current login session.

Dimming adds a temporary dark overlay. It does not change hardware backlight brightness or HDR settings. Use Restore All Brightness to remove every overlay. All overlays disappear when the app quits.

Choose any of the six languages in Settings, or follow your system language. No account or sign-in is required. If a change fails, use macOS System Settings > Displays. Contact support with your macOS version, display model and connection type; avoid sending personal or confidential information.
